I am a Research Fellow at [Input Output](https://iohk.io/) and an Honorary Fellow at the [Department of Computer Science](https://www.durham.ac.uk/departments/academic/computer-science/research/groups/), Durham University, where I am a member of the [NESTiD](https://nestid.webspace.durham.ac.uk) and [ACiD](https://algorithmscomplexity.webspace.durham.ac.uk) groups. I was an Associate Professor at Durham from 2022 to 2025.

Before that, I was a lecturer (Assistant Professor) at the University of York and Queen's University Belfast, and a research scientist in the [Crypto Team](https://crypto.di.ens.fr/web2py) at École Normale Supérieure, Paris. I held postdoctoral research positions at Royal Holloway, University of London, and in the [Cryptoplexity Group](https://www.cryptoplexity.informatik.tu-darmstadt.de/) at TU Darmstadt.

I completed my PhD at the [Cryptography and Information Security Group](http://www.bris.ac.uk/engineering/research/cryptography/), University of Bristol, under the supervision of Prof. Nigel P. Smart, and studied Mathematics to Part III at [Queens' College](https://www.queens.cam.ac.uk/), University of Cambridge.
